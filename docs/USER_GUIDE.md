# SmartGov Health Portal - User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to the SmartGov Health Portal. This platform is designed to provide comprehensive, accessible information about healthcare schemes in Andhra Pradesh. 

## Key Features

1. **Language & Accessibility**
   - Use the **Aa** button in the header to increase or decrease text size.
   - Use the **🌗** Contrast button to switch between Light, Dark, and High Contrast modes.
   - Use the **English / తెలుగు** toggle to switch the interface language instantly.

2. **PWA Installation**
   - Click the **📲 Install** button at the top right to install the application to your home screen or desktop.
   - Once installed, the app functions smoothly even offline!

3. **Browsing Schemes**
   - The main dashboard displays a grid of available health schemes.
   - Use the **Search** bar to quickly find schemes (e.g., "pregnancy", "Aarogyasri").
   - Click any scheme card to view detailed eligibility, benefits, and required documents.

4. **Audio Assistance**
   - Many schemes feature a pre-generated high-quality Telugu audio guide.
   - If offline audio is unavailable, click the **🔊 Read Aloud** button to use the browser's built-in text-to-speech.

5. **AI Healthcare Advisor (Chat)**
   - Click the floating **💬 (Chat)** button in the bottom right corner to open the AI assistant.
   - You can type questions like "Am I eligible for free dialysis?" and the AI will analyze your query and suggest relevant schemes.

6. **Document Upload (OCR & Vision)**
   - You can upload a government GO, a health certificate, or any scanned PDF (up to 20MB).
   - Our advanced AI will extract the relevant information and provide a simplified summary in English and Telugu.

## Offline Capabilities
SmartGov is an **Offline-First** application. Once you load the website for the first time, all static assets and the entire scheme catalog are saved to your device. You can disconnect from the internet and still read about all health schemes!

## Troubleshooting
- **Infinite Loading?** We've implemented 20-second timeouts. If your internet is extremely slow, the request will cancel gracefully and alert you to try again.
- **AI Features Offline?** The AI chat and Document Simplifier require an active internet connection. If you are offline, these features will display an error, but the scheme catalog remains available.
